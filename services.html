Formats

Three ways to work together

Independent evaluation

Is it working?

“We rolled out AI coding assistants. Are we faster, and is quality holding?”

Scope
AI tool rollouts, process changes, training or innovation programmes
Method
Baseline and follow-up metrics, developer surveys, interviews, repository data
You get
Evaluation report with measured effects, risks and a recommendation
Typical
4–8 weeks
Technical assessment

Are we ready?

“How should we bring LLMs into our product, and what does the EU AI Act ask of us?”

Scope
GenAI and RAG readiness, engineering practices, responsible AI governance
Method
Workshops, architecture and practice review against published evidence
You get
Assessment, prioritised roadmap and a half-day team workshop
Typical
2–4 weeks
R&D collaboration

Can we build this together?

“We have an innovation idea and need a research partner and funding.”

Scope
Research Council of Norway, Innovation Norway, Interreg and Horizon Europe projects
Method
Joint proposal writing, work-package lead, industrial or public-sector PhD
You get
A funded project with a research team and documented results
Typical
2–4 years

Topics

What the work usually covers

Any of these can be scoped as an evaluation, an assessment or a longer collaboration.

Generative AI in engineering

  • Measuring the effect of AI coding assistants on speed and quality
  • Integrating LLMs and RAG into products and services
  • Team training on AI fundamentals and practical use
  • Responsible AI: bias, transparency, fairness and accountability

Software development practice

  • Adapting agile frameworks such as Scrum or Kanban to your context
  • Quality assurance, automated testing and continuous integration
  • Process improvement to reduce waste and rework
  • Managing technical debt and planning refactoring

Startups and innovation

  • Prototyping, MVP planning and product validation
  • Business model and growth strategy for software ventures
  • Evaluating incubator, accelerator and innovation programmes
  • Sustainability goals in entrepreneurial projects
