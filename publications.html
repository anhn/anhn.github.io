Research

Empirical software engineering research

I lead the Management Information Systems research group at the University of South-Eastern Norway, with an affiliation at NTNU. My work studies software startups, generative AI in software engineering and responsible AI in practice.

Google Scholar ↗ORCID ↗DBLP ↗Software Startup Research Network ↗

Software startups

Engineering and innovation under extreme uncertainty: validation, product-market fit, early-stage methods.

Generative AI in software engineering

How LLMs change developer tasks, productivity and quality, and how to evaluate them.

Responsible AI in practice

Turning ethics and regulation into concrete engineering practice, including inclusive AI for elderly and neurodiverse users.

Supervision and teaching

7 PhD candidates (5 completed), including industrial and public-sector PhDs, and 20+ MSc and BSc students since 2015. Current courses: TDT4242 Advanced Software Engineering (NTNU), MSM4102 Green IT and GIS and PRO1000 Practical Project Management (USN).

Positions

Professor, USN (since 2022) · Associate Professor 20%, NTNU (since 2021) · Visiting Professor, Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (since 2020). PhD, NTNU 2015.
